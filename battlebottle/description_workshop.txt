[h1]Battle Bottle[h1]
[h2]Reusable Buff Potions[h2]

This mod adds the Battle Bottle, an item that adds a small additional inventory for buff potions. When one is in your inventory and there is a boss fight or select event going on, you will have those buffs applied to you constantly without consuming the potions. 


You can unlock more slots for your Battle Bottle by defeating vanilla optional bosses, going from 3 to a maximum of 8. 


The Battle Bottle can be obtained pre-boss, with the following recipe: 
1 Bottle + 1 Obsidian Skull + 3 Fallen Stars + 1 Demonite/Crimtane Bar @ a demon alter / crimson alter. 


This should be compatible with all modded boss fights and buff potions, but only modded events that are considered invasions as there is no universal event flag.
